Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Who can register on StudentHub?

All enrolled students of FTE (IT, CSE, CE, EC) can create an account using their college enrollment ID.

Q2: Is registration free for hackathons and workshops?

Most campus events are completely free for CHARUSAT students. Some specialized certified workshops may have a small material fee.

Q3: How do I get participation certificates?

After the event coordinator verifies your attendance, certificates will be visible on your student dashboard.

Q4: What should I do if I forget my password?

You can contact the department lab assistant with your student ID card or use the password reset link on the login page.
