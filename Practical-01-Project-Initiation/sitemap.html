StudentHub Portal - Sitemap

Hierarchical view of all planned pages for the StudentHub semester project.

1. Public Pages (Accessible by all users)

  • Home Page (index.html) - Welcome banner, quick announcements, featured events.
  • About Us (about.html) - FTE departments, faculty info, vision & mission.
  • Events Catalog (events.html) - List of upcoming workshops and hackathons.
  • FAQ Page (faq.html) - Frequently asked student questions.
  • Contact Us (contact.html) - Campus contact info and inquiry form.
  • Feedback Form (feedback.html) - Student suggestions and rating.

2. Authentication Pages

  • Student Registration (register.html) - Form to create a new student account.
  • Login Page (login.html) - Sign-in for registered students and admins.

3. Student Portal Pages (After student login)

  • Student Dashboard (dashboard.html) - Registered events, attendance overview.
  • Student Profile (profile.html) - Academic details, branch, semester.

4. Admin Portal Pages (After admin login)

  • Admin Dashboard (admin.html) - Overview of student count, event count, and quick links.
